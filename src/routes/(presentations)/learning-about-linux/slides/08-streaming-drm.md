## Streaming and DRM

- Digital rights management
    - Controls access to protected media
- Widevine is Google's DRM system
    - Available in Linux browsers
- Streaming services set the limits
    - Resolution, HDR, and downloads
    - Depends on browser, operating system, and hardware
- Codecs handle encoding and decoding, not access

---

## Netflix on Linux

- Up to 1080p in supported browsers
    - Includes Chrome and Firefox
